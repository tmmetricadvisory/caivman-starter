# Your skills

A skill is the written method for one job: what comes in, what goes out, what a good one looks like, and where Claude must stop and ask. Each one is a folder here with a `SKILL.md` inside. Claude reads the right one when the job comes round.

The first-skill plugin builds your first one from a job you already do and one example you were proud of. After that, ask Claude: "What else do I do every week that could be a skill?"

Skills live in the brain, so they travel with it: a second computer, or a second person, gets them too. Corrections you make while running a skill are filed back into it at wrap up.
