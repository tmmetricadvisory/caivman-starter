# The bench

Raw material for capture. Drop files here; Claude reads them once and writes its notes into `context/`. Nothing in this folder goes to GitHub, so a colleague you invite later never sees your sent items.

- `best-work/`: three pieces you were proud of, the originals. Capture writes `context/standards.md` from them.
- `voice/`: ten sent emails that sound like you on a good day, as files or pasted into one text file. Capture writes `context/voice.md` from them.

If the tool your documents live in is already connected, you can skip the bench and tell Claude where they are instead. It records the place in `context/sources.md`.
